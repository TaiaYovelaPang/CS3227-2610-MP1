# Appendix: Requirements

## Product Scope

**Target user:** Team managers who coordinate training sessions, members, duties, and competitions.

**Value proposition:** 
- Link a Google Sheet to track member attendance
- Ability to customise duties needed
- Generate fair duty rosters from confirmed attendance
- Review attendance statistics
- Maintain important team dates, and receive timely reminders
